Download CVDownload CV

Graphic Eye, Product Mind

I came to product design from graphic design, so the visuals are never an afterthought. Every flow starts with research and user needs, and every screen is crafted down to the type, color and spacing. The result is a product that works well, looks considered and stays consistent as it grows

AI in the Loop

AI is built into my process: it speeds up research, structure, copy drafts and code, while every design decision stays mine. That frees time for what AI can’t do: understanding users, finding the real problem and polishing the details. Careful craft at startup speed, without cutting corners
