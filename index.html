About

Where I Come From

Born and raised on Gotland, Sweden, I learned the value of adaptability and collaboration early. These traits still guide my approach: listening actively, asking the right questions, and empowering others to succeed. Based in Stockholm for most of my career, I have also worked closely with teams in Oslo, London, Tel Aviv, New York, San Jose, and Kuala Lumpur.

Get ResumeGet Resume

Why I Design

I’m passionate about turning vision into outcomes by aligning design with business goals, user needs, and team growth. For me, design is not just about creating, it’s about enabling meaningful change.

What I Bring

For over 20 years, I’ve focused on creating meaningful user experiences, meeting people where they are and building shared understanding. My work spans advising stakeholders, leading design teams, and contributing hands-on as a UX/Product Designer.
